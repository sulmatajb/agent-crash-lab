# Recorded UI direction
The actual app is the visual identity: pale gray canvas, white evidence panels, dark green sidebar, lime product mark. The recording fills the frame. Do not turn it into slides, statistics, or typographic storytelling.

One focal area at a time: connection, live tool timeline, timeout, ledger reconciliation, one payment and receipt, checks. Crop and zoom with smooth camera movements; preserve readable source UI and enough surrounding context to orient the viewer. No decorative effects. Inter is already bundled locally for the few editorial labels. The primary visual is the actual app recording at every moment.
